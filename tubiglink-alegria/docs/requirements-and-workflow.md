## 2. Request workflow (as built)

1. **Resident submits** (barangay, purok/sitio, problem, duration, household size, contact or landmark). Receives a reference number.
2. **Coordinator verifies** within their barangay: check urgency and possible duplicates (the app flags them, staff decide). Statuses: *Under verification → Verified*, or *Referred / Unable to fulfill* with a note the resident can read.
3. **Municipal team assesses** demand against confirmed available water and sets *Scheduled / In progress*.
4. **Assistance or referral**: a delivery is scheduled, or the concern is referred to the provider or the responsible office.
5. **Outcome recorded**: *Resolved* (with a note) or it stays open as *Referred* / *Unable to fulfill*. Nothing is closed silently, and only *Resolved* leaves the outstanding list.

Statuses: Submitted · Under verification · Verified · Scheduled · In progress · Resolved · Referred · Unable to fulfill.
A referral is not a resolution. Unmet demand stays visible in reports.

## 5. Gap to say out loud

TubigLink coordinates assistance and **records unmet demand**. It does not create water. Shortages caused by sources, pipes or missing connections need action from the proper offices and providers, and the reports exist to make that case.
